4-API fallback chain. Rates always available.

FX Currency tries four live rate APIs in order. If one fails it jumps to the next instantly — so you get live rates, not an error screen. The converter at the top of this page uses the same idea.

Never an error screen. If all four APIs are down, the app uses 114 built-in fallback rates and a banner tells you exactly how old they are.

Feature set 05

Questions? We got answers.

Is FX Currency actually free?
Yes — 100% free forever. All 114 currencies (including crypto), all 5 screens, rate alerts, trip budgeting, streaks, achievements, live news and offline support are free. FX Currency Pro is an optional upgrade for power users who want unlimited history and advanced analytics.
Does it work offline?
Yes. A service worker caches the last rates it fetched. When you’re offline the app shows those cached rates and clearly labels how old they are. 114 built-in fallback rates (including BTC, ETH and BNB) mean you never see an error screen — even on first launch with no internet.
How often are rates updated?
Every 5 minutes while the app is open. FX Currency uses a 4-API fallback chain — if the first API fails, it tries the next one instantly. Crypto rates (BTC, ETH, BNB) come from CoinGecko and are kept when fiat rates refresh.
What’s the streak system?
Like Duolingo, but for currency! Make at least one real conversion a day to keep your streak alive. You earn XP, unlock 15 level titles, collect achievement badges, and the Josh mascot reacts to your progress with 6 different moods. You earn a streak freeze every 7 days (max 2), and there’s a native Android home-screen widget.
What platforms is FX Currency on?
FX Currency is on the Google Play Store for Android, and can be installed as a PWA on any device with a modern browser. iOS support is planned. It’s a Capacitor-wrapped web app, so it also runs in any Chromium-based browser.
